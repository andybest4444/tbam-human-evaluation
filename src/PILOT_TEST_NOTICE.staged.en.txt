TBAM Anonymous Route Human Evaluation: Internal Evaluation Notice and Consent

This is a small internal anonymous route evaluation, not public recruitment. After direct identifiers are excluded, your A/B choices and response times will be aggregated with other evaluators' results for research analysis and may be reported in a paper only in anonymous, aggregate form. We do not request and should not collect your real name, email address, student ID, employee ID, or any other direct identifier.

Each item shows two anonymous complete route maps. Based on the same natural-language task instruction, choose which route is better overall, Route A or Route B. Do not separately judge whether a route completed the task, assign dimension scores, or use a tie option. The maps show elevation, cover, complete paths, and time markers spaced proportionally across each map's horizon. The complete design is frozen in advance at 50 maps and 300 comparisons. Independently verified items are added to the catalog in waves. The first wave contains 50 items; later waves may only add items and may never remove, replace, or change an item you have rated. You may work over multiple sessions and continue after a new wave with the same browser, pseudonym, and PIN. You may reopen a submitted item and replace your earlier choice; the result export retains only your latest submitted choice for each item.

The page stores your A/B choices, response times, pseudonym, and recovery data in this browser. GitHub Pages does not receive or store these evaluation responses, although GitHub records Pages visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. When finished, you must download the “results and progress JSON” and send it to the study coordinator. Do not send the full browser backup, which contains pseudonym and PIN-recovery information, as the result file. Clearing this site's browser data may delete progress that has not been exported.

Use only a pseudonym that contains no direct identifying information. Participation is voluntary, and you may stop at any time. If you stop and do not send the result JSON, the page will not automatically transmit your responses to the researcher. If you encounter a problem with the route maps, text, saving, recovery, or export, record the affected item number and report it to the study coordinator.

By checking the consent box, you confirm that you have read and understood this notice, volunteer to participate, and agree that the anonymous evaluation result you actively return may be used for aggregate research and paper analysis.
